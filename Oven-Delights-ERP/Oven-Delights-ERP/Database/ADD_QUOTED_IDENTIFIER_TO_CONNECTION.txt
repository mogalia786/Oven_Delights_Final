SOLUTION: Add QUOTED_IDENTIFIER=ON to your connection string in App.config

Find your connection string in App.config and add this parameter:

BEFORE:
<connectionStrings>
    <add name="OvenDelightsERPConnectionString" 
         connectionString="Server=your-server;Database=OvenDelightsERP;User Id=user;Password=pass;" 
         providerName="System.Data.SqlClient" />
</connectionStrings>

AFTER:
<connectionStrings>
    <add name="OvenDelightsERPConnectionString" 
         connectionString="Server=your-server;Database=OvenDelightsERP;User Id=user;Password=pass;QUOTED_IDENTIFIER=ON;" 
         providerName="System.Data.SqlClient" />
</connectionStrings>

Add: QUOTED_IDENTIFIER=ON; to the end of your connection string.

Then rebuild and test.
